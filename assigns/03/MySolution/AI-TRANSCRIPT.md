## Corrections and Review
I did not let the AI edit my files directly. I used many of its ideas and copied its format, though. 

To review the AI's output, I compared its suggestions against the provided stakeholder brief and the assignment requirements. In general, I checked that:

- Requirements were supported by statements in the stakeholder brief or were clearly identified as assumptions
- Requirements covered the major behaviors described by the stakeholder
- Requirements were separated from assumptions and unresolved questions
- Requirements did not introduce unnecessary implementation details

The final requirements specification is therefore AI-assisted, but I reviewed the suggested requirements and acceptance criteria against the assignment instructions and stakeholder brief before including them.
